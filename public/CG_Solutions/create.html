Building Your Dreams

We’ve gone through your design and have everything just the way you want it. You survived the seemingly endless process of getting everything perfect. Our hope is that the rigorous and exhaustive design we’ve developed will minimize, if not eliminate, the headache that so many hear of during construction. So, where do you go from here?

Structural Calculations

Every design is checked by our licensed Civil Engineer, but we leave the numbers to the Structural Engineer. For a small fee, they can check your design and we can make any changes they recommend.

Find the Right Builder

Get the right person for the right job with a guarantee to finish the work to your satisfaction. We can help to answer any design questions and coordinate any changes they have.

Construction Loan

For those who are looking for the funding, your builder can help you set up a payment and inspection schedule to help them get started on the work.

Obtain the building permit

Every local city or county requires the work have a permit so a qualified inspector can help ensure a safe and quality home. We can provide you with everything they need to issue that building permit.
