Explore Your Style

New Construction or Remodel, Your Way

We like to meet in your house to get an idea of what you like, your lifestyle and how you use your home. We’ve found that Pinterest and Houzz provide great ideas and clues to how you want things to look and function, so we’ll work with you to get a feel for what you want.

We have families and busy lives just like you and we understand that your time is precious. If you would rather set up a remote meeting online, we can step through our entire process through the comfort of your own computer.

Some Basic Topics We'll Discuss
  • - Land or Property - There are many variables that could affect the cost of the home based on which lot is chosen or where you need that remodel.
  • - Home and Construction Style - Traditional, modern, ranch or rambler, the list goes on and on.
  • - Plans or layouts - Maybe you love the layout of your sister's house or just found the perfect plan that just needs some slight changes.
    • - We can help to point out ways to save on your dream design or to keep costs in line with your budget.
  • - Budget - Most people have no idea how much construction really costs; we work with you to help create a comfortable budget that matches your needs.
