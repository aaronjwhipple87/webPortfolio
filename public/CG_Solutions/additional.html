Additional Services

We offer even more than an awesome design experience. In addition, there are other areas that tend to get overlooked where we can help.

Site Survey

So you found the perfect lot for your perfect home? No worries, we can digitize every hill and vale on that lot and figure out the best way to place your home to catch the morning rays or make sure you don’t have to show the nightly Netflix sessions with your neighbors. Just to be clear, we are not licensed surveyors that determine property boundaries. We will look up the county records to determine the legal descriptions, but our purpose is to provide a surface to place your home on. We aren’t quite cool enough to be official surveyors yet. Before you click away, though, rest assured knowing your data is collected and then checked by a licensed Civil Engineer.

Landscape Layout

No amazing remodel or new home would be complete without that landscape design that just makes everything look perfect. To be honest, most local nurseries and landscape companies offer low cost or even free landscape designs, and they’re usually a great start. We can pull in every plant they recommend and not only provide a layout plan and plant schedule but also a visualization of what it will look like the day you plant or ten years from now, making sure you put the right plants in the right places now. If you want us to start from scratch and begin the design and then carry it through the process, we can help there as well. As with everything else, the decision is completely up to you. Here again, we are passionate about landscape design, but we are not licensed landscapers, we’ve designed (and installed) a lot of landscapes, but we are not cool enough to be professional landscape architects yet.

Irrigation Implementation

Here again, we’ll be up front and honest; you can go to your local plumbing supply store and they do a real thorough job of figuring all the parts and pieces to keep every plant healthy and happy for FREE. In our experience, they do a decent job, and you can end up with almost everything taken care of. We take this a step further as well and electronically measure and layout every single rotor, pop-up, drip, and pipe to minimize materials and water usage while maximizing your coverage and satisfaction with or without someone else’s design.

And how much do we charge for all this?

  • - Site data collection - $0.037/sf (roughly $1600/acre)
  • - 3D surface creation and home configuration options - $400
  • - Landscape Plan and Plant Schedule - $400 (from scratch) / $200 (optimization only)
  • - Landscape Visualization (Current and future plant size) - $200
  • - Irrigation Design - $200 (from scratch) / $100 (optimization only)
